# MyIsland - Native Dynamic Island (Advanced Feature Modules Built)

We have built and integrated all requested advanced feature modules into **MyIsland**.

---

## 🚀 Advanced Features Delivered

1. **iOS-Style Top Volume & Ringer Island HUD ([VolumeRingerManager.kt](file:///c:/Users/Deval%20Shalkhlan/Desktop/reactApp/MyIsland/app/src/main/java/com/myisland/dynamic/service/VolumeRingerManager.kt))**:
   - Intercepts side volume key presses and ringer mode changes, rendering a top-mounted volume slider pill popping out directly from your camera cutout.

2. **In-Island Quick Reply for WhatsApp & Telegram**:
   - Tapping "Reply" on an expanded notification reveals an inline text input box and Send button directly inside the island card.

3. **Lockscreen & Always-On Display (AOD) Support**:
   - Extended overlay window flags (`FLAG_SHOW_WHEN_LOCKED`) so ongoing music controls, timers, and incoming call alerts stay active on your lockscreen.

4. **Radial Long-Press Quick Action Palette**:
   - Long-pressing the island opens a 4-button quick-action wheel (Audio Output Switcher, Torch, Mute Notifications, Close).
